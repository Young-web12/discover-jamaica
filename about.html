About the Project

Learning about Jamaica through tourism

Our Purpose

This school tourism website introduces students to Jamaica's attractions, culture and history. It demonstrates how HTML5, CSS3, multimedia, JavaScript and responsive design can be combined to create a useful real-world website.

  • Encourage interest in Jamaican tourism.
  • Support classroom research and presentations.
  • Promote respect for local culture and the environment.
  • Present information in a clear, accessible format.
