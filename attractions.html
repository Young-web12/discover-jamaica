Jamaican Attractions

Explore places that make Jamaica special

Dunn's River Falls

A famous waterfall attraction near Ocho Rios where visitors can enjoy the natural scenery and climb the falls with guides.

Blue Mountains

A scenic mountain region known for beautiful landscapes, hiking and Jamaica's world-famous coffee-growing areas.

Jamaican Beaches

Jamaica has many beaches where visitors can relax, enjoy water activities and appreciate the island's coastline.

Responsible tourism: Protect natural areas, dispose of waste properly, respect local communities and follow attraction safety rules.
